# Sounds

All fourteen sounds in this folder are from the pack **RPG Audio** by Kenney (Kenney Vleugels, www.kenney.nl), licensed
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: free for personal and commercial projects, no credit required, given here anyway.

- Pack: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (the pack's own `License.txt` says the same)

The files are the pack's own `.ogg` files, copied unchanged (not edited, not re-encoded) and renamed:

| File here | File in the pack | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `footstep_01.ogg` to `footstep_10.ogg` | `footstep00.ogg` to `footstep09.ogg` | a footstep (ten variants, played at random without repeating one twice in a row) |
| `door_open.ogg` | `doorOpen_1.ogg` | a door swings open |
| `door_close.ogg` | `doorClose_1.ogg` | a door swings shut (its thud is 0.2 s into the file) |
| `lock_click.ogg` | `metalClick.ogg` | a locked door is pushed |
| `key_pickup.ogg` | `handleCoins2.ogg` | the player picks up a key |
